## 1. The service

Relay is a Jira app that lists open issues whose assignee is away, suggests
successors, and hands issues over (reassign + handover comment) when a user
confirms.

## 2. Use

Relay is provided as-is, without warranties of any kind. Handovers change
real Jira assignments, so always confirm the successor before executing a
handover.
